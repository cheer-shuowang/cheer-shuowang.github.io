01 / About

A little about me

I'm Shuo (Allen) Wang, a master's student at the National University of Singapore. My research connects human–computer interaction, computational social science, and the digital humanities. I use NLP, machine learning, and LLM-based simulation to understand social behaviour, and build interactive systems to explore new research questions.

At NUS, I work with the CALM Lab on AI for historical texts and collaborate with AERO Lab on a serious game about energy and comfort. Earlier roles at Bilibili, Tencent, and Weibo shaped my interest in how platform design influences behaviour.

Outside work: travel, the violin, and a fair number of hours in the games I also study.

  • HCIHow people perceive and act inside designed systems — online communities, games, and buildings.
  • CSSComputational social science — polarization, risk governance, and urban life.
  • DHDigital humanities — computation and multimodal AI applied to cultural and historical texts.
